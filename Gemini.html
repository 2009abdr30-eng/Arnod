Protocol & Poster Audit

Why Arnold × Upper/Lower

Running a 6-day Arnold split across 5 days leaves one muscle group at 1x/week. Combining 3 Arnold days (Mon–Wed) with Rest (Thu) and Upper/Lower (Fri–Sat) delivers an exact 2x stimulus to every muscle.

Sub-60m Antagonist Pacing

Take 2–3 min rest on primary barbell squats, deadlifts, bench, and rows. Pair opposing movements (Incline Press + Pulldown, Curl + Extension) with 30–45s swaps to cut ~14 minutes per workout.

4-Step Double Progression

Keep weights steady and add 1+ clean reps weekly at 1–3 RIR. Once you hit the top of the rep range on all sets (e.g. 8, 8, 8), add +2.5 kg (5 lb) upper or +5 kg (10 lb) lower next session.

SKINNY BULKING GUARDRAILS

Nutrition, Sleep & Deload Rules

Caloric Surplus (+300–400 kcal/day)

Cap scale gain at ≤2% bodyweight/month for novices (≤1% intermediate). If scale weight stalls for 2 consecutive weeks, add +150–200 kcal/day.

Macro Distribution

Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg across 3–5 meals; Carbs 4–6 g/kg (include 25–40 g carbs + protein 60–90m pre-workout); Fats 0.6–0.9 g/kg.

7+ Hours Nightly Sleep

Growth hormone release, glycogen resynthesis, and neural recovery depend on 7+ hours of sleep across 5 weekly sessions.

Scheduled Deload (Every 4–6 Weeks)

Reduce working weights to ~60% (or cut working sets in half) for 1 week whenever joint fatigue accumulates or reps stall.
